# Atelier Vintage Design System & Tokens
> Synthesized from Inspo MCP references: Buly 1803 (`buly1803-com`), Maharishi (`maharishistore-com`), Artek (`artek-fi`) and Criterion (`criterion-com`).

## Visual Aesthetic & Tone
An archival, tactile, and editorial vintage luxury experience. Ample negative space, high-contrast serif headlines paired with tracked utilitarian sans-serif, warm museum parchment surfaces, delicate 1px borders, and authentic metadata stamps (archival registration IDs, year stamps, provenance details).

## Color Palette

### Ground & Paper Bands
- `--bg-parchment`: `#F9F7F2` (Warm archival museum paper ground)
- `--bg-parchment-subtle`: `#F2EFE8` (Slightly deeper paper for cards & wells)
- `--bg-canvas`: `#EBE7DD` (Dividers, borders, muted surfaces)
- `--bg-dark-accent`: `#181614` (Deep obsidian/charcoal for badges & dark mode accents)

### Typography & Inks
- `--text-ink-primary`: `#1C1917` (Rich warm black / deep umber)
- `--text-ink-secondary`: `#57534E` (Muted warm graphite for subtitles and metadata)
- `--text-ink-tertiary`: `#8C857B` (Subtle archival stamp text, index numbers)

### Accents & Patina
- `--color-terracotta`: `#A65B32` (Vintage wax seal, warm terracotta accent)
- `--color-antique-gold`: `#C4975A` (Curator stamp, highlight bronze)
- `--color-forest`: `#2D4736` (Deep botanical green for "Certified Authentic" seals)
- `--color-ruby`: `#8E2B2B` (Archival red stamp, rare edition indicator)

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Serif**: `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Cinzel`, or `serif` (High contrast, editorial tracking, wide headers)
- **Monospace / Archival Stamp**: `JetBrains Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `monospace` (For catalog numbers, dates, serials)
- **Clean Interface Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `system-ui` (Legible, crisp micro-copy and navigation)

## Rhythms & Borders
- Border radius: `0px` to `2px` for a crisp, archival paper feel. Tactile, not bubbly.
- Border stroke: `1px solid rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)`
- Vertical section spacing: `clamp(64px, 8vw, 120px)`
- Horizontal container: `max-w-7xl` (1440px max content width with `px-6 md:px-12`)
